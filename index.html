// 01 · THE PROBLEM

The algorithm was never built for your book.

Mainstream marketing treats every novel as the same product with a different cover. Yours isn't. Here is what actually breaks — and what we build instead.

PROBLEM / A

Ads to Amazon are first-date proposals.

You pay to send a stranger to a storefront that also sells ten thousand lookalikes — and the reader is gone before the sample loads. We point ads at a page you own, with one promise and one field.

PROBLEM / B

Mainstream targeting flattens culture into keywords.

"Fantasy" is not an audience. The reader searching for Assamese forest spirits is not the reader searching for dragon academies. We target the shelf your book actually lives on.

PROBLEM / C

You lose the reader the moment you lose the address.

An algorithm can demote you overnight. An email list is property — portable, exportable, yours. Every system we build hands you the keys on day one.

PROBLEM / D

Hype decays. Respect compounds.

Countdown timers and artificial engagement are rented attention. A reader who chose your world freely, and was welcomed properly, stays. That is the entire strategy.

// 05 · THE OPERATOR

Ten years in publishing, one conclusion.

First as a publicist, then as an editor, Emily watched the same story repeat: the books with the most to say received the least machinery. The deeply cultural ones were marketed with stock ad templates written for anything. In 2022 she left to build that machinery herself.

Today Emily Editorial is a studio of four, working with a deliberately small roster — six authors at a time — building reader systems for fiction rooted in myth, memory and the speculative traditions mainstream marketing can't see. Every engagement runs entirely in writing.

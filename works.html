// 03 · THE STUDIO

Every build, the same three promises.

Whether the book is a Kashmiri literary epic or a cozy robot novella, the machinery never changes its manners. The studio stays small so the work stays careful.

Four people. Six authors at a time. Everything in writing, forever.

You own the list.Every address lives in an account in your name, exportable from day one — including the day you leave.
No calls, ever.Proposals, builds, launches and reports all happen by email, so you can keep writing.
